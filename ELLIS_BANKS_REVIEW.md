## Week 1 — How much

- **portion** *(noun)* — A portion is one part or share of something larger. · *Synonyms:* share, part, serving · *Root:* Latin portio — a share
  - Ratchet gave every Autobot a small portion of energon before the long trip.
  - I ate a big portion of spaghetti at dinner.
  - *Family:* proportion, proportional, apportion
- **scarce** *(adjective)* — If something is scarce, there is very little of it and it is hard to find. · *Synonyms:* rare, limited, scanty
  - Energon became scarce on Cybertron, so the Autobots had to share it carefully.
  - Fresh water is scarce in the desert.
  - *Family:* scarcity, scarcely, scarcer
- **numerous** *(adjective)* — Numerous means many or very many. · *Synonyms:* many, countless, plentiful · *Root:* Latin numerus — number
  - Optimus Prime stayed calm even though numerous Decepticons gathered on the ridge.
  - The sky was full of numerous stars last night.
  - *Family:* number, numeral, numerical, innumerable
- **sufficient** *(adjective)* — Sufficient means as much as you need. · *Synonyms:* enough, adequate, ample · *Root:* Latin sufficere — sub (under) + facere (to make)
  - Wheeljack checked that the Autobots had sufficient energon for the trip home.
  - Is there sufficient paper for the whole class?
  - *Family:* suffice, sufficiency, sufficiently, insufficient
- **supply** *(noun / verb)* — A supply is an amount of something kept for later use, and to supply is to give someone what they need. · *Synonyms:* stock, store, provide · *Root:* Latin supplere — sub (up) + plere (to fill)
  - Ratchet kept a large supply of repair tools inside the Ark.
  - The school will supply pencils for the big test.
  - *Family:* supplier, supplies, supplement
