# Ellis — Conventions bank (review sheet)

Iowa Level 10 format, built 2026-10-05 from his placement check (spelling 2/6, capitals 1/6 → both start at stage 1). **Mon/Wed/Fri spelling** (four words, find the one spelled wrong), **Tue/Thu capital letters** (three lines, find the one with a capital-letter mistake). Every item ends with (No mistake), and about 1 in 7 really has none. Spelling ladder follows All About Spelling: weeks 1–6 Level 1 patterns (held longer, on purpose) · 7–12 Level 2 · 13–19 Level 3 · 20–25 Level 4 + grade-4 classics. No misspelled word is ever a word on his Level 1 list; every misspelling was checked against the dictionary so none is a real word. Capitals ladder: weeks 1–6 names and I · 7–12 days/months/holidays/places/titles · 13–18 titles of works, languages, history · 19–25 the over-capitalisation traps. **Each capitals item prints its rule family on his page** (placement showed he can't spot an error for a rule nobody has stated); the full rule prints in your Teaching Companion. ✔ marks the answer. Nothing here is loaded or live yet.
