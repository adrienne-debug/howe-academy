## The core flag

`core:true` = the card is named by a Learning Objectives line or by a 2026 Area 8 test question in COVERAGE_objectives.md.
Matching is by the exact card front as written in the coverage maps. ID decks: the objective lines name whole species sets, not
single cards, so ID cards are core by attribute — Wildlife: `· class`, `· food`, `· habitat` (the three every animal has on the
teacher's sheet); Aquatic: classification / family / habitat / adult food / native status / pollution-tolerance group; Forestry:
every tree card ("identifying features of species in the designated set"); Soils: the horizon cards ("Identify parts of a soil
profile"). CEI and the Appendix have no objectives sheet, so only their test-backed cards are core — the teacher may want to
treat the whole CEI Soil Health group as core. Nothing was dropped: `core:false` cards are the rest of the manuals' terms and facts.

## Why the set is bigger than the 900–1,300 the plan estimated

### Forestry

I checked the `in` hints and also grepped every glossary headword against the manual, as the lead asked.
- **Aggregate:** the glossary sense ("to gather into a group or mass") is not how the manual uses the word. The manual's "aggregate fruit" is carded from p.68 instead.
- **Arrangement:** the glossary sense is the wildlife-habitat element. The manual uses "arrangement" for leaves and twigs, plus one loose "in an arrangement" on p.12.
- **Blind:** the only match is "blinds" (window blinds, p.11). False match.
- **Codominant:** the glossary sense is a characteristic species of a community. The manual uses "co-dominant" as a crown class, carded from p.32.
- **Crop:** the glossary sense is a bird's crop. The manual means farm crops.
- **Cross Section:** the glossary sense is "a sample meant to represent a whole". The manual means a cut surface of a trunk.
