This document has three parts: what is there today (Part 1), the plan (Part 2), and the one step that is built on this

Lucy's notebooks and every notebook setting in the app, counting with small scripts. I spot-checked their findings.
Labels: **[fact]** = read in the code. **[my read]** = my judgement. **[measured]** = rendered in Chrome and measured.

## 5. What makes Julian's, Caleb's and 3D's interchangeable — and what does not

**Interchangeable today [fact]:**
- Any Pre-K kid can wear any look at any level. Look and level are stored apart.
- The name printed and traced is its own setting ("3D" prints as the name you typed).
- Unit pages, the spelling page and the "pages today" strip follow the look's colors.
- A kid with no notebook of their own (3D) gets the Pre-K notebook as soon as a look is picked.
- Picking a look changes the **next** notebook built. A week already sent to a tablet is a stored copy; it does not change.

**Not interchangeable yet:**
- The three borrowed pieces and the counting picture (§4 rows 1–4).
- Julian's and Caleb's first looks are theirs alone (typed names, their own guides).
- Julian has his own planner card; every other Pre-K kid gets the shared one.
- The Review-slots stepper shows for Julian only (another session is fixing this).
- Only Mom can pick. There is no kid-side control anywhere.
- An older kid's notebook cannot take a look at all (§6).

**Two required, thirteen optional.** A look can be as small as a palette and a mascot.

**The rule that keeps it safe:** a look may only *fill a slot*. It can never add a page, remove a page, or change a
question, a level, a letter, a number or a word of the day. The test for every look is the one this branch adds: same
picks, same pages in the same order, the same page skeleton — and every page measured.

**The hard case, said plainly.** Lincoln's and Ellis's *built-in questions* are themed (snake sentences, brand
stories). A look must not touch a question. So a Lincoln who switches to Space keeps snake sentences until his built-in
banks are replaced. Your own banks already replace them box by box, and the two "ladder" sessions are building shared
banks. My advice: leave the questions alone, and let the themed built-ins retire as your banks and the ladders arrive.

## 12. Who picks

**You** decide which looks a kid may use. **The kid** picks from those, on their own device. It changes the **next**
notebook built — never a week already printed or sent to a tablet.

| Control | Its ONE home | Everywhere else |
|---|---|---|
| **Looks this kid may use** (tick list) | Notebook tab ▸ Customize ▸ Look | — |
| **Which look is on** — kid may choose (2+ ticked) | Kids' Corner ▸ "🎨 My notebook look" | Notebook tab shows "Picked: 🚀 Space" read-only |
| **Which look is on** — kid may not choose (0–1 ticked) | Notebook tab ▸ Customize ▸ Look (as today) | Kids' Corner shows nothing |

So there is never a moment when two places can both change it.

**What the app needs:**
1. A tick list of allowed looks per kid. Saved one tick at a time. Nothing ticked = today's behavior.
2. A Kids' Corner card: big picture buttons (mascot, picture row, a color swatch) so a non-reader can choose. It saves
   only the look, only for the kid whose device it is, only a look you ticked. The line under it reads: "Your **next**
   notebook will be 🚀 Space."
3. A small picture of each look, drawn from the look's own pieces, so the card never needs a look typed into the app.
4. For older kids: Stage A first. Until their notebook can read a look, the card has nothing to offer them.

The app already works this way for games: you switch games on per kid, the kid chooses among them, and the kid's
thumbs-up is saved as "a note to Mom".

**"Never a week already printed."** A tablet week is a stored copy, so it is safe today. Paper is safe because it is
paper. One gap: if you *reprint* the same week after a kid changes look, the reprint comes out in the new look (same
work, new pictures). Question 6 asks whether that matters.
