# Lincoln — Conventions bank (review sheet)

Iowa format, built 2026-09-21. Mon/Wed/Fri spelling (four words, find the one spelled wrong), Tue usage (three lines, find the one with a mistake), Thu mixed. Every question ends with **No mistake**, and about 1 in 7 really has none. Spelling ladder: weeks 1–5 patterns he has finished in AAS (Level 1 + Level 2 to step 15) · weeks 6–15 the 6th-grade patterns · weeks 16–25 the classic test traps. Every misspelling was checked against the dictionary so none is a real word. ✔ marks the answer; the rule prints in your Teaching Companion. Nothing here is loaded or live yet.
